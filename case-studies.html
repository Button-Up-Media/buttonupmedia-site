Organic Social Instagram & TikTok

The Fatty Crab — Organic Social Performance

Objective

Build a consistent, brand-safe organic social presence that drives awareness, captures attention, and nurtures an audience that regularly visits The Fatty Crab in person.

Strategy

  • Defined content pillars around food, experience, people, and culture
  • Created short-form video templates for fast production and editing
  • Optimized posting schedule based on audience behavior and local patterns
  • Layered in captions, hooks, and CTAs tailored to reservations and visits

Impact

The Fatty Crab's social channels evolved from static posts and inconsistent cadence to a high-performing organic engine that keeps the brand top-of-mind and feeds every part of the funnel: awareness, consideration, and repeat visits.

Organic Social Instagram Reels

The Fatty Crab — Uber Eats Organic Traffic

Objective

Use organic social — specifically a single, well-crafted Instagram Reel — to drive Uber Eats organic traffic without additional paid budget.

Strategy

  • Scripted a short-form narrative around "order tonight" urgency
  • Showcased pack-out visuals and hero dishes to increase craving and click-through
  • Included clear "Order on Uber Eats" CTAs in the caption and on-screen text
  • Timed the post to align with peak delivery hours for maximum intent

Impact

By pairing platform-native creative with a direct delivery CTA, The Fatty Crab was able to move audiences from discovery to Uber Eats orders using purely organic reach — no incremental paid budget required.

Influencer & Organic Social July 2023 – March 2024

The Fatty Crab — Influencer Campaign

Objective

Partner with the right creator to introduce The Fatty Crab to new, high-intent audiences and convert that attention into followers, engagement, and in-restaurant traffic.

Campaign Design

  • Identified a local food creator with a strong Miami/South Florida audience
  • Co-developed content concepts around hero dishes, ambience, and staff hospitality
  • Scheduled posts for peak discovery windows across Instagram and TikTok
  • Supported the campaign with reposts, story features, and community management

Impact

The influencer activation generated an 'instant marketing moment' for The Fatty Crab: highly shareable content, a surge of new followers, and a noticeable uptick in guests mentioning the creator's content when visiting the restaurant.
