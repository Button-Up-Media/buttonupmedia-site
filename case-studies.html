Toni's Sushi Bar

  • South Beach, Miami Beach (1208 Washington Ave)
  • Family-run sushi bar since 1987
  • Recently onboarded
South Beach · Since 1987 Toni's Sushi Bar @tonissushi on Instagram

The challenge

Toni's has been family-run on South Beach since 1987, when Hiromi “Toni” Takarada opened it, according to Miami New Times. A spot with that much history already has the food and the story. The work is getting both in front of people who now choose where to eat by scrolling short videos.

What we did

  • Made the sushi chef the star. He is the on-camera personality, so every video feels like a seat at the bar.
  • Close-ups that make people hungry: knife work and nigiri, shot up close.
  • Hooks written the way people scroll, like “POV: you find the best sushi spot on South Beach.”

Results

The Fatty Crab

  • Doral (2000 NW 87th Ave)
  • Cajun seafood boil
  • Influencer campaign Jul 2023 to Mar 2024

The challenge

The feed was mostly static posts on an inconsistent schedule. A Cajun seafood boil is made for video, and the occasional photo was not keeping The Fatty Crab top of mind.

What we did

  • Four content pillars: the food, the experience, the people and the culture, so every post has a job.
  • Short-form video templates for fast shooting and editing.
  • A posting schedule tuned to when their audience is online, on Instagram and Facebook.
  • Captions, hooks and calls to action aimed at one thing: reservations and visits.
  • An “order tonight” Reel. One organic Instagram Reel with pack-out shots and an “Order on Uber Eats” call to action, posted at peak delivery hours. It drove Uber Eats orders with no paid budget behind it.
  • An influencer campaign (July 2023 to March 2024). We partnered with a local food creator with a strong Miami and South Florida following, planned the videos together around hero dishes, the room and the staff, posted at peak discovery times on Instagram and TikTok, and backed it up with reposts, story features and community management.

Results

21M+Organic views
110K+Followers
6.5%Engagement rate

Engagement rate is how often people react to posts with likes, comments and shares. The influencer campaign also brought a wave of new followers, and more guests mentioned the creator's videos when they came in.

Rusty Pelican Miami and Tampa

  • Miami (on the water, Key Biscayne) and Tampa
  • Google Ads, 2025

The challenge

Big waterfront rooms live on reservations, private events and holiday rushes. In two very different markets, Miami and Tampa, every ad dollar has to show what it brought back.

What we did

  • Google search ads aimed at people already looking for a place to eat, with every click tracked through to the booking.
  • Separate campaigns for reservations, private events and seasonal dates, with private events as the focus in Tampa.
  • Full conversion tracking on calls, reservations and form fills, reported against spend every month.
  • The website, too. We designed and built the Rusty Pelican site, with full-bleed photography, a menu by section, the chef's story and reservations wired in. More on our restaurant website design page.

Results

How to read these numbers: a conversion is a tracked action, like a reservation, a call or a private-event inquiry. ROAS (return on ad spend) compares tracked conversion value with ad spend, so 7:1 means about $7 of tracked value for every $1 spent.

The Boathouse and The Odyssey

  • Private events and dinner reservations
  • Google Ads, 2025

The challenge

A private event is a big-ticket booking: each one that converts can bring in thousands. The goal is to reach people who are already planning an event and turn their search into an inquiry, not to buy as many clicks as possible.

What we did

  • The Boathouse, a landmark waterfront restaurant: branded search ads for private events. Branded search means ads that show when someone searches for the restaurant by name, so the restaurant's own ad is the first thing a planner sees.
  • The Odyssey: campaigns for both dinner reservations and private events.
  • Castaway: campaigns for reservations and private events.
  • Tracking on every inquiry and booking, so each campaign is judged on what it brings back.

WOA Cocktails

  • Miami and New York
  • Luxury mobile bar and beverage company

The challenge

WOA sells a premium experience, and the website needed to feel just as premium. Customers and press needed a home online that matched the quality in the glass.

What we did

  • Rebuilt the site from scratch: fully custom and hand-coded, with immersive visuals, smooth motion and a mobile-first layout.
  • Every page in English and Spanish, with a one-tap language toggle.
  • Set up so Google indexes both languages, so WOA shows up whether someone searches in English or in Spanish.
