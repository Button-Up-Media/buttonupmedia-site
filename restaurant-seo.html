Three places diners look for a restaurant

Someone deciding where to eat usually checks one of three places: Google search, Google Maps or an AI assistant. Each one takes different work, and we handle all three.

What AEO looks like for a restaurant

Some diners skip the search results and ask ChatGPT or Google's AI where to eat. The answer names a few places, with a short reason for each, based on what the assistant can find about them online.

That's why the details matter. If your hours aren't listed, or your website never mentions the patio, the assistant has nothing to go on.

In this example, the answer came from:

  • Covered patio and group reservationsYour website
  • Open until 11 PMYour Google Business Profile
  • Friendly staffYour reviews

Questions about restaurant SEO and AEO

What is restaurant SEO?

It's the work that helps your restaurant's website show up when someone searches Google for a place to eat, like “ramen Downtown Miami”. It covers the words on your pages, your menu page, site speed and the code that tells Google you're a restaurant. For local searches, your Google Business Profile and reviews matter just as much.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimization. It's the work that helps AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI answers find accurate information about your restaurant and include you when someone asks where to eat. For a restaurant, that means the same facts everywhere, clear answers on your website and good reviews.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO is about getting your website higher in Google's list of search results. AEO is about getting named in the answer an AI assistant writes, where there may be room for only a few restaurants. Most of the work overlaps, so we do both together.

Why does my Google Business Profile matter so much?

It's the listing that shows on Google Maps and in the local 3-pack, the three restaurants Google shows first for searches like “restaurants near me”. Diners check your hours, photos, stars and menu there before they ever open your website.

Do you manage my Google Business Profile?

Yes. We fix your categories, hours, menu and reservation links, add new photos and posts, and reply to reviews in English or Spanish. The profile stays yours.

Can you get my restaurant recommended by ChatGPT or Google's AI?

No one can guarantee it, so be wary of anyone who does. What we do is make you easy to recommend: the same facts on every listing, clear answers to common guest questions on your site, structured data and mentions on trusted local sites. That also helps AI tools describe you correctly.

How long does restaurant SEO take to work?

We don't promise timelines, because it depends on your area, your competition and what's already in place. Google profile and listing fixes are usually the first changes you'll notice. Website rankings and AI answers build over time, and your monthly report shows what moved.

How much does restaurant SEO cost?

We set pricing on the strategy call, based on your market, your number of locations and what's already in place. Most restaurants invest $1,500 to $5,000 a month across a coordinated marketing program, with no long-term contracts.

Do you work with restaurants outside Miami or outside Florida?

Yes. We're headquartered in Miami, and our SEO, Google Business Profile and AEO work is done remotely, so we work with restaurants anywhere in the US. We work in English and Spanish.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. We don't require long-term contracts. Pricing and what's included are set on the free strategy call, based on what your restaurant needs.

Do you do SEO in Spanish?

Yes. Our team is bilingual, so we research Spanish searches, write Spanish pages and answer Spanish reviews in Spanish. In Miami those searches are easy to miss when your website only speaks English.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not always. We start with the site you have, and if your platform blocks the basics (editable page titles, a real menu page, decent speed on a phone) we'll tell you straight.

Do you work with restaurant groups and multiple locations?

Yes. Each location gets its own Google Business Profile work, its own page on your website, its own listings cleanup and its own line in the monthly report.

Do I keep everything if I stop working with you?

Yes. Your Google Business Profile, your website changes, your listings and your reports stay yours.
