Three places diners find a restaurant, and what wins in each.

  1. 1 2 3

    Google MapsThe “map pack” for searches like “sushi near me”

    What wins: a complete, active Google Business Profile with fresh photos, steady reviews and correct hours.

  2. Google search resultsThe links under the map, for searches like “ramen Downtown Miami”

    What wins: a fast website that says what you serve and where, with a real menu page.

  3. AI answersChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, asked “where should I eat tonight?”

    What wins: the same facts everywhere, plus clear answers on your site that AI tools can repeat.

In Miami, all three happen in English and in Spanish. Getting AI tools to describe you correctly is called answer engine optimization (AEO).

Local keywords and pages

Your site never says what you serve and where, so Google can't match you.

What we do

  • Find the searches you can win in English and Spanish, like “ramen Downtown Miami”.
  • Put them in your page titles, headings and menu.

Website speed and structure

A slow site and a PDF menu that phones and search engines struggle to read.

What we do

  • Speed up your site on phones and shrink heavy images.
  • Turn your menu into a real web page.
  • Add schema markup, the code that tells Google “this is a restaurant, here are the hours”.

Listings consistency

Yelp, Apple Maps and the delivery apps show a different name, phone or hours.

What we do

  • Match your name, address, phone and hours everywhere.
  • Cover Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, Resy and the delivery apps.
  • Remove duplicate and closed listings.

Reviews

Reviews trickle in on their own, and the bad ones go unanswered.

What we do

  • Make it easy for happy guests to leave a review. Google says review count and rating factor into local ranking.
  • Reply to every review, in English or Spanish.
  • Never buy reviews or ask only the guests we know are happy. Google's policies ban both.

AI-answer readiness

ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews repeat old facts about you, or skip you.

What we do

  • Keep the same facts everywhere AI tools read.
  • Answer common guest questions on your site: parking, dress code, kids, private rooms.
  • Add structured data and earn mentions on trusted local sites.

Is restaurant SEO right for you?

A good fit if

  • You search your cuisine and neighborhood and a competitor shows up first.
  • Your Google profile has barely changed since you opened.
  • You want bookings that don't stop when ad spend does.
  • You run a restaurant, bar, cafe or a group with several locations.

Not the right fit if

  • You need full tables this weekend. SEO builds over months; ads work faster.
  • You want to set it once and forget it.
  • You're not in hospitality. Restaurants, bars and cafes are all we do.

Restaurant SEO questions, answered.

What is restaurant SEO?

It's the work that gets your restaurant shown when people nearby search for a place to eat on Google, Google Maps and AI tools like ChatGPT. It covers your Google Business Profile, your website, your listings and your reviews.

Why does my Google Business Profile matter so much?

It's the listing that shows on Google Maps and in the map pack, the three restaurants Google shows first for searches like “restaurants near me”. Diners check your hours, photos, stars and menu there before they ever open your website.

How long does restaurant SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile and listing fixes usually move first. Website rankings build month over month, so think in quarters, not weeks.

How much does restaurant SEO cost?

We set pricing on the strategy call, based on your market, your number of locations and what's already in place. Most restaurants invest $1,500 to $5,000 a month across a coordinated marketing program, with no long-term contracts.

Do you do SEO in Spanish?

Yes. Our team is bilingual, so we research Spanish searches, write Spanish pages and answer Spanish reviews in Spanish. In Miami those searches are easy to miss when your website only speaks English.

Can you get my restaurant recommended by ChatGPT or Google's AI?

No one can guarantee it, so be wary of anyone who does. We make your facts match everywhere AI tools look, answer common guest questions on your site and add structured data, which makes you easier to recommend and to describe correctly.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not always. We start with the site you have, and if your platform blocks the basics (editable page titles, a real menu page, decent speed on a phone) we'll tell you straight.

Do you work with restaurant groups and multiple locations?

Yes. Each location gets its own Google Business Profile work, its own page on your website, its own listings cleanup and its own line in the monthly report.

Do I keep everything if I stop working with you?

Yes. Your Google Business Profile, your website changes, your listings and your reports stay yours.
