Why we started

We give hospitality owners an affordable way to stand out, so they can focus on the craft while we focus on selling it.

Chris and Juan started Button Up Media in 2022 with one stubborn idea: that great restaurants, bars, and coffee shops shouldn't get lost in some of the densest competition in the country just because they don't have time to market themselves. So we built the agency to carry that weight, with affordable marketing that sets you apart while you pour your energy into your product, your team, and your dishes.
