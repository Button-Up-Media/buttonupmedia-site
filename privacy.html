Privacy Policy

How Button Up Media uses and protects information

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Button Up Media collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our websites, submit a form, contact us, or interact with our marketing and advertising channels. It is written to reflect common U.S. privacy law requirements and should be read together with any consent choices you make through our cookie banner or forms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, business name, and any message you send us.
  • Business information: details about your restaurant, services requested, budget range, or project goals.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, device type, browser type, pages visited, referring URLs, time spent on pages, and similar analytics data.
  • Cookie and similar technology data: consent preferences, session identifiers, and analytics or advertising signals, where enabled.
  • Communications: records of emails, calls, form submissions, and appointment requests.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • Provide restaurant marketing services and manage client relationships.
  • Improve our website, measure performance, and understand what content is useful.
  • Run and evaluate advertising, including remarketing or audience measurement where enabled.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Send marketing communications when permitted by law and by your preferences.

3. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our site, measure traffic, and support marketing performance. You can manage your cookie choice through the cookie banner shown on our site. If you reject non-essential cookies, we will respect that choice for the current session, and we may ask again in a later session depending on your selection.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, host forms, send email, schedule appointments, measure analytics, and run advertising campaigns. We may also share information when required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger or sale.

We do not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws treat certain advertising or analytics disclosures as a "sale" or "sharing." Where those laws apply, you may have the right to opt out through your privacy choices or by contacting us.

5. Email Marketing and CAN-SPAM

If you subscribe to updates or receive marketing emails from us, you can opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe instructions in the message or by contacting us directly. We will not send spam knowingly and we aim to honor unsubscribe requests promptly.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we maintain about you. You may also have the right to opt out of targeted advertising, certain disclosures of personal information, or profiling where those rights apply under state law.

California residents and residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws may have additional rights under applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy rights allowed by law.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our websites and services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

8. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. When we make updates, we will post the revised version here and update the date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to exercise your privacy choices, contact us at contact@buttonupmedia.com or call (786) 740-9498.

This policy is provided as a general website privacy statement and should be reviewed by counsel before use in production.
