What you get

What we'd do for your restaurant

We handle the filming, the plan, the posting and the replies. Our team is headquartered in Miami, and we also work with restaurants outside South Florida.

  • 01

    Filming at your restaurant

    In South Florida, our own crew comes to your restaurant and films your food, drinks, kitchen and dining room. Outside South Florida, the shoot is done by an experienced videographer or photographer from our network in your area. One shoot typically produces 4 to 6 weeks of content.

  • 02

    A monthly content calendar

    Our Miami team plans what to post around what's happening at your restaurant, like a new menu, a seasonal special, an event or a slow night you want to fill. You approve the calendar before anything goes out.

  • 03

    Posting on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook

    We write the captions in your restaurant's voice and post everything on your calendar, with hashtags and location tags that help people nearby find you.

  • 04

    Comments and DMs every day

    We answer comments and DMs every day, including reservation questions, in English, Spanish or both, so your followers hear back and nothing gets missed.
