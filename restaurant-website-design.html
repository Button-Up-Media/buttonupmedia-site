FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions about our restaurant website design, packages, integrations, and support.

01

Do I need to be a restaurant to work with you?
Our restaurant website design packages are built for restaurants and food & beverage businesses, but we also work with hospitality brands broadly: cocktail bars, mobile bars, catering companies, coffee shops, and similar businesses. If you're not sure if you're a fit, book a call.
02

What if I already have a website?
We almost always recommend a restaurant website redesign from scratch rather than retrofitting an existing site. Retrofits take longer, cost more, and usually result in a weaker final product. We'll discuss your situation on the kickoff call.
03

Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Payment plans are available on both restaurant website build options, so you do not need to pay in full upfront.
04

What POS systems do you integrate with?
Toast, Square, Clover, and most other major restaurant POS platforms. If you use something unusual, we'll confirm compatibility on the kickoff call.
05

Can I add or remove things after launch?
Yes. The Monthly Care Plan includes up to 5 content edits per month and 3 landing page builds per quarter, which covers most ongoing restaurant website support needs. For larger changes, we can scope additional work separately.
06

Do I own the website when it's finished?
Yes. You own everything we build: domain, code, content, and assets.
