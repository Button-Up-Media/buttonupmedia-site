The Problem

Why Most Restaurant Websites Don’t Convert

We’ve audited hundreds of restaurant websites. The same problems show up again and again:

The Menu Is Impossible to Find

The most visited page on your website is buried, broken, or invisible to Google.

  • Buried behind 3 clicks or hidden in a hamburger menu
  • Uploaded as a blurry PDF that can’t be read on mobile
  • Not optimized for Google — Google can’t read PDFs
What should happen:

Menu visible in one tap. HTML-formatted so Google can index every item. Mobile-readable without pinching and zooming.

The Mobile Experience Is Broken

Over 75% of restaurant website traffic comes from mobile devices. Most restaurants were designed desktop-first.

  • Tiny text and slow load times on phones
  • Buttons too small to tap accurately
  • Forms that don’t work on mobile browsers
What should happen:

Mobile-first design where every element is built for thumbs, not mouse clicks. Fast load on cellular networks.

There’s No Clear Call to Action

Customers land on the site and have no idea what to do next.

  • No reservation button above the fold
  • No “Order Online” link without scrolling
  • No clickable phone number on mobile
  • No clear path from interested to committed
What should happen:

Primary CTA (reserve, order, call) visible within 2 seconds of landing on any page. No scrolling required.

The Design Is Outdated

Your website looks like it was built in 2017. Customers notice.

  • Stock photos instead of real food photography
  • Inconsistent branding across pages
  • Walls of text with no visual hierarchy
What should happen:

A modern, visually driven design that showcases your actual food, your actual space, and your actual brand.

No SEO — Google Can’t Find You

Your website exists but Google doesn’t know what to do with it.

  • No title tags or meta descriptions
  • No schema markup telling Google “this is a restaurant”
  • No keyword targeting for cuisine + location
  • No structured data for menu items, hours, or reviews
What should happen:

Every page built with SEO fundamentals so Google knows what you serve, where you are, and why you should rank.

It’s Slow

The average restaurant website takes over 5 seconds to load. Every second costs you customers.

  • Every second over 3s loses 7% of visitors
  • Slow sites rank lower on Google
  • Bloated code and uncompressed images
What should happen:

Page load under 3 seconds on mobile. Core Web Vitals optimized. Images compressed. No bloated code.
