SEO

SEO is included in every build

A good-looking website only brings in bookings if people can find it. Every site we build is set up for Google search and for AI assistants like ChatGPT.

  • Google search

    Pages are set up to show up when diners search for menus and places to eat nearby.

  • AI assistants

    Pages are structured so ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews can read what your restaurant offers and describe it correctly.

  • Location pages

    Pages for your neighborhood or area help you show up in local searches, where it's easier to rank than for broad terms.

  • Schema markup

    Code that tells search engines your cuisine, hours, location and reviews.

  • Fast load times

    Lean code and compressed images keep pages fast on phones.

  • Clear, readable content

    Copy and headings are organized so both people and AI assistants can follow them easily.

Included in every package

There's no separate SEO tier to buy. These basics come with every build, because a restaurant website needs them to get found.

  • Keyword research before we write any copy
  • Page titles, meta descriptions, schema markup and clean URLs
  • Content aimed at the area you serve
  • Copy that AI assistants can understand and cite

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about our restaurant websites, pricing and support.

How much does a restaurant website cost?
Our Standard website build is $1,500 and the Premium integrated build is $2,000, both as one-time payments. Optional add-ons are a content shoot for $750 ($500 when added to Premium) and a monthly care plan for $250 a month. Payment plans are available on both builds.

Do I own the website when it's finished?
Yes. You own everything we build, including the domain, code, content and assets. If you ever stop working with us, you take all of it with you.

Can you build a website for a restaurant outside Miami or Florida?
Yes. We build websites remotely, so we can work with restaurants anywhere in the US. Our team is headquartered in Miami, and every step, from the kickoff call to launch, works the same wherever your restaurant is.

Is every page in English and Spanish?
Yes. Every site we build has each page in both English and Spanish, with a language toggle on every page. We write and set up both versions, and Google indexes them separately, so you can show up in searches in either language.

What if I already have a website?
We'll usually recommend rebuilding it from scratch instead of reworking your current site. Reworking an old site tends to take longer and cost more, and the result is usually weaker. We'll look at your current site and talk it through on your free strategy call.

Can you connect OpenTable or online ordering to my site?
Yes. We connect reservations, online ordering and POS systems, including OpenTable, Toast, Square and Clover. The Standard build includes menu, booking and order links, and the Premium build adds POS menu sync, a reservations integration and ordering support.

What POS systems do you integrate with?
We work with Toast, Square, Clover and most other major restaurant POS systems. If you use something less common, we'll confirm it works on the kickoff call.

Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Payment plans are available on both website builds, so you don't have to pay in full upfront.

Can I add or remove things after launch?
Yes. The monthly care plan includes up to 5 content edits a month and 3 landing-page builds per quarter, which covers what most restaurants need after launch. Bigger changes can be quoted separately.

Do I need to be a restaurant to work with you?
Not necessarily. Our website packages are built for restaurants, and we also work with cocktail bars, mobile bars, catering companies, coffee shops and similar food and drink businesses. If you're not sure you're a fit, book a call and ask.

Who will I talk to on the free strategy call?
You'll talk to Juan, our co-founder, not a sales rep. The call is free and takes 30 minutes. Juan looks at your current site and your goals and tells you what we would build.
