Questions

Questions about the restaurant marketing grader

What is a restaurant marketing grader?

A restaurant marketing grader is a free tool that scores how your restaurant shows up online and tells you what to fix first. This one grades six areas: your website on a phone, your food photos, your Google Business Profile and reviews, your ordering and reservation setup, your Instagram and TikTok, and your on-page SEO. It then compares you with the restaurants near you and gives you one score out of 100 with a list of fixes in order.

Is the restaurant website grader free?

Yes, completely. There is no trial, no card and no account to create. To unlock the full report we ask for your phone number, so we can send you your results and follow up about them. You can reply STOP at any time.

What does the grader check on my restaurant’s website?

Mobile load speed and Core Web Vitals (we run Google’s own PageSpeed Insights, so the numbers match what Google sees), whether the layout holds up on a phone, whether your menu and prices are easy to find, whether there is a working order or reserve button, whether you own your domain, and on-page SEO like your title tag, H1, meta description, image alt text and social share tags.

How does the social media grader score my Instagram and TikTok?

It finds your public Instagram and TikTok profiles from your website and your Google listing. Then it looks at your followers next to restaurants near you, how many posts you have, how recently you last posted, and whether your bio shows your location, hours and a link to order or book. It flags things that cost you customers, like an account that has gone quiet for two months, lots of posts with almost no followers, or several accounts for the same restaurant.

Do I have to connect my Instagram or Google account?

No. Nothing gets connected and nothing gets logged into. The grader reads public information only, the same things a customer looking you up would see.

Does it check my Google Business Profile and reviews?

Yes. It scores your star rating, your review count, the tone of your recent reviews, how many photos are on the listing, whether your hours and phone number are filled in, and whether your website is linked. It also shows how you compare with restaurants near you.

How long does the scan take?

About 60 seconds. Most of that is the live website speed test, which runs in real time against your site rather than pulling a cached score.

How is this different from a generic website grader?

A generic website grader only scores a web page. Guests often find you on Google, in your reviews or on Instagram before they ever see your website, so this grader covers all of it, and it compares you with real restaurants near you instead of an industry average.

Does it work for bars, cafés, coffee shops and food trucks?

Yes. Anything with a Google Business Profile works. If your spot is not listed, you can grade it by website address instead.

Where does the restaurant marketing grader work?

The scan works anywhere Google has a listing for your business. Button Up Media is headquartered in Miami, FL, and we work with restaurants anywhere in the US, so the free call is open to you wherever you are.

What happens on the free 30-minute call?

You talk to Juan, our co-founder, not a sales rep. He goes through your report with you and tells you what he would fix first. The call is free, takes 30 minutes on Google Meet or by phone, and there are no long-term contracts if you decide to work with us.
