FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions restaurant owners ask us most.

01What does a restaurant marketing agency do?
A restaurant marketing agency helps restaurants bring in more guests through social media, websites, search and ads. At Button Up Media that means shooting photos and short videos, running your social media pages, building your website, working on your Google Business Profile, SEO and AEO, and managing Google, Instagram and Facebook ads. We only work with restaurants, bars and cafes.
02How much does restaurant marketing cost?
Most restaurant owners invest between $1,500 and $5,000 per month for marketing that covers several channels. The price depends on how much content you need, which services you choose and your ad budget. The free strategy call is the easiest way to get a number for your restaurant.
03What is the best marketing strategy for a restaurant?
For most restaurants, the best strategy combines a few channels that support each other: regular short videos on Instagram and TikTok, a website where people can see the menu and book, a complete Google Business Profile, and ads aimed at people nearby who are deciding where to eat. Where to start depends on where you're losing guests today.
04How do I get more customers to my restaurant?
Make it easy for people to find you, like what they see and book a table. People check Google, Google Maps, Instagram and TikTok before they choose where to eat, so it helps to have a complete Google Business Profile, regular video content, local ads and a website that works well on a phone with a clear reservation or order button.
05Why hire a restaurant-specific marketing agency?
Restaurants are marketed differently than most businesses. People decide where to eat quickly, mostly from photos, videos and reviews, and your competition is often a few blocks away. An agency that only works with restaurants already knows what content brings people in and how to plan around slow and busy periods.
06What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) for restaurants?
AEO is the work of getting your restaurant mentioned when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews where to eat. It comes down to clear, accurate information about your restaurant on your website, your Google Business Profile and your listings, plus structured data and strong reviews. AEO is part of our SEO and AEO service.
