FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about restaurant marketing and working with Button Up Media.

01What does a restaurant marketing agency do?
A restaurant marketing agency specializes in helping restaurants increase their visibility, attract more customers, and grow revenue through digital marketing. This typically includes social media management and content creation, website design and optimization, search engine optimization (SEO), and paid advertising on platforms like Google and Facebook. Unlike general marketing agencies, a restaurant marketing agency understands the unique challenges of the hospitality industry, thin margins, high competition, and the constant need to keep seats filled.
02How much does restaurant marketing cost?
Restaurant marketing costs vary depending on the services you need and the size of your operation. At Button Up Media, we build custom strategies tailored to each restaurant's goals and budget. Most restaurant owners invest between $1,500 and $5,000 per month for a coordinated marketing strategy that includes multiple channels. The best way to get a clear picture is to schedule a free consultation so we can understand your specific situation and recommend the right approach.
03What is the best marketing strategy for a restaurant?
The most effective restaurant marketing strategy is an integrated one, not just social media or just a website, but all channels working together. At a minimum, a strong restaurant marketing strategy should include an active social media presence with professional video content, a mobile-optimized website that makes it easy to view menus and book reservations, local SEO so you appear when customers search for restaurants nearby, and targeted advertising to capture high-intent customers. The restaurants that grow fastest are the ones that coordinate these channels into one unified system.
04How to get more customers to my restaurant?
Getting more customers starts with making sure people can find you online. Over 97% of diners use Google to find restaurants, and social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok directly influence where people choose to eat. To get more customers, you need to be visible when they search, look compelling when they find you, and make it effortless to book or order. That means optimizing your Google Business Profile, posting engaging video content consistently, running targeted local ads, and having a fast, mobile-friendly website with clear calls to action.
05Why hire a restaurant-specific marketing agency?
Most marketing agencies use the same playbook for restaurants that they use for dentists, gyms, and law firms. Restaurants are different. The decision happens in seconds, it's driven by photos and reviews, and the competition is the restaurant down the street. A restaurant-only agency knows how menus actually sell, why people pick one spot over another on a Friday night, and how to plan around the slow and busy seasons in your market. That knowledge shows up directly in better content, smarter strategy, and faster results.
06What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) for restaurants?
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of setting up your restaurant's online presence so it shows up in AI search results, including Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT. When someone asks an AI tool "where should I eat tonight?", AEO is what gets your restaurant in the answer. This involves structured data, entity optimization, strong review signals, and writing content AI tools can actually read and cite. Button Up Media builds AEO into every restaurant marketing plan from day one.
07Do you work with restaurants outside of Florida?
Button Up Media is headquartered in South Florida and serves restaurants across the state, from Miami and Fort Lauderdale to Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Naples, and beyond. While our deepest expertise is in the Florida market, we also work with restaurant brands in other states. If you're interested in working together, the best first step is to schedule a free consultation so we can discuss your needs and see if we're the right fit.
